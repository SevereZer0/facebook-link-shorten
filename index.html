Legacy Link Converter

Paste any Facebook URL. The original path, query, and hash are preserved and rewritten for Facebed. Numeric story.php and permalink.php links are shortened to legacy /posts/ links when possible. Conversion happens only in your browser.

Supported formats

All URLs on facebook.com and its subdomains are accepted, including common forms such as:

/reel/VIDEO_ID/watch/?v=VIDEO_ID/USERNAME/videos/VIDEO_ID/photo/?fbid=PHOTO_ID/share/.../groups/GROUP_ID_OR_SLUG/posts/POST_ID/groups/GROUP_ID_OR_SLUG/permalink/POST_ID/USERNAME/posts/POST_ID_OR_PFBIDstory.php?story_fbid=POST_ID&id=OWNER_IDpermalink.php?story_fbid=POST_ID&id=OWNER_ID

Unknown or future Facebook routes are passed through unchanged except for the domain, so they do not need individual support rules. Numeric legacy post URLs are still normalized to /OWNER_ID/posts/POST_ID where possible.
