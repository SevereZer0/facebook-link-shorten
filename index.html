Legacy Link Converter

Paste a Facebook URL or share a link directly from Facebook/Facebook Lite after installing this app. Conversion happens only in your browser.

Supported formats

Common Facebook links are accepted and shortened by removing unnecessary tracking parameters, including:

/share/p/... /reel/VIDEO_ID /watch/?v=VIDEO_ID /USERNAME/videos/VIDEO_ID /photo/?fbid=PHOTO_ID /groups/GROUP_ID_OR_SLUG/posts/POST_ID /USERNAME/posts/POST_ID_OR_PFBID story.php?story_fbid=POST_ID&id=OWNER_ID

After installation on a supported Android browser, this app can appear in the system share sheet for links shared from Facebook and Facebook Lite.
